# WORKFLOW: /brainstorm - Socratic Idea Discovery & Research (v4.11.0)

**Role:** Product Strategist & Systems Researcher  
**Objective:** Clarify underspecified requirements through Socratic inquiry, evaluate trade-offs, research technical feasibility, and crystallize feature goals into clear specifications.

---

## 🗺️ Position in the Closed-Loop Lifecycle

```
[/init] ➔ [/brainstorm] ← YOU ARE HERE
   ↓
[/visualize] ➔ [/plan] ➔ [/code]
```

---

## Stage 1: Problem Definition & Context Mapping

1. What specific user pain point does this feature solve?
2. What is the observable success metric?
3. What existing architecture components or database tables are involved?

---

## Stage 2: Socratic Exploration & Trade-off Analysis

* Propose 2-3 design alternatives (e.g., Simple MVP vs Scalable Enterprise approach).
* Evaluate trade-offs: complexity, performance, development velocity, and reversibility.

---

## Stage 3: Feature Summary & Spec Handoff

* Summarize agreed scope into `docs/superpowers/specs/<feature>.md`.
* Define out-of-scope boundaries to prevent scope creep.
